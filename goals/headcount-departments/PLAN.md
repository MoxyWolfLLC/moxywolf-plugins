# headcount-departments
1. Import finance and people
   - for each of `finance` and `people`, every `skills/<skill>/SKILL.md` and `skills/<skill>/references/sources.md` under `plugins/<department>/` in `cbrock84/headcount` at `98d1c17` is present at the same path in this repository and identical to upstream, except that each backticked reference of the form `<department>:<skill>` naming a department this marketplace does not carry becomes plain words naming the function, with no address; no `.codex-plugin` folder is copied
   - `plugins/<department>/.claude-plugin/plugin.json` has `name` equal to the department, `version` `0.1.0`, upstream's description, `author` `MoxyWolf LLC`, and `repository` `https://github.com/cbrock84/headcount`
   - `plugins/<department>/LICENSE` is upstream's MIT license text verbatim, copyright line included
   - `plugins/<department>/README.md` names the source repository, the commit `98d1c17`, the MIT license, that the skills were copied, and every edit made to them; it lists the department's skills and its agent
   - `plugins/<department>/agents/<department>.md` has frontmatter `name` equal to the department and a `description` of 1024 characters or fewer that says what work to delegate to it; its body tells the agent to load `finance:chief-financial-officer` (or `people:chief-human-resources-officer`, for its own department) first, then the specialist skill in the department that matches the request, to stay inside the department's remit and name the right department for anything outside it, to cite what it used from that skill's `references/sources.md`, and to return the answer, the skills it loaded, its sources and its open questions
   - `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json` gains one entry per department with `name` the department, `source` `./plugins/<department>`, `version` `0.1.0` and the plugin.json description; its top-level version and every existing entry are unchanged, and its diff shows only the added entries
   - `python3 plugins/gstack-execution/scripts/skill_packaging.py --json`, run from the repository root, prints to standard output a check with status `pass` for every imported SKILL.md and exits 0; it writes nothing to standard error
   - `python3 plugins/gstack-execution/scripts/run_all_tests.py` exits 0 in CI and reports a nonzero count of what it examined
