I found one contradiction and several ambiguities. All of them sit in ME-004. I found no contradiction among the constraints: *One release path*, *Coverage is mandatory*, the gate, the goal rules and *Test the boundaries* agree with each other.

**Contradiction**
- **C1 (ME-004.2, ME-004.4, ME-004.1):** ME-004.4 says reads and writes both 0 are "reported as below the cache minimum". ME-004.1 and .2 let a job record 0/0 when no caching was requested, for example a `"cache": false` job or a non-Anthropic job. If the live deliberation includes such a job, rule 4 forces a report that the job was "below the cache minimum", which is false.
  - **Smallest change:** limit the 0/0 rule to jobs that sent `cache_control`. Report jobs without it as "caching not requested".

**Ambiguities**
- **A1 (ME-004.3, ME-004.2):** "The field… its absence" doesn't say whether the test checks the request's `cache_control` or the recorded `cache`.
- **A2 (ME-004.2, *Links introduced*):** `cache` is declared as `{read, write}`, but ME-004.2 adds `cache.reason`. ME-004.2 doesn't say what `reason` holds when both fields are absent, when only one is, or when neither is. EV-001 is cited but not stated.
- **A3 (ME-004.4):** The mixed cases aren't classified. One example is reads 0 with writes `null`.
- **A4 (ME-004.4):** It's unclear whether the verdict is per job or per deliberation, given that `_summary.json` carries `cache` per job.
- **A5 (ME-004.5, *Shared by ME-002 and ME-003*):** It's unclear whether the top-level marketplace version moves once or twice.
- **A6 (*Shared by ME-002 and ME-003*, ME-004.3):** It's unclear whether the `run_all_tests.py` clause applies to ME-004's new test. Registering the test might mean editing `run_all_tests.py`, which is a `CODEOWNERS` path (*The pre-merge gate is GitHub's*). That would require Dorian's approving review instead of a merge by clean review (*The agent merges on a clean review*).
