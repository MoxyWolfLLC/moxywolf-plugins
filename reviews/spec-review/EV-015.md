# Spec review: DESIGN.md EV-015

_2026-11-16T07:07:24+00:00 · claude-sonnet-5-5 with claude-fable-5-2 advisor (1 calls)_

I found no contradictions and four ambiguities. The advisor confirmed the first one and flagged the other three pairs.

1. **EV-015.3 (alone).** "Check 3's rule" for a `knownIssues` source refers to a check the plan never defines. It could also be a slip for criterion 3 itself. The rule has to be stated or cited.
2. **EV-015.4 with EV-015.5.** EV-015.4 says the repairs run "only through the skill". EV-015.5 says the skill is "the only copy of the checks" and says nothing about repairs. The plan doesn't say whether the scheduled-prompt pointer, or the canonical copy in `references/`, may keep any repair wording. "Runs no checks or repairs" only covers the case where the skill is missing. A reader can't tell what the pointer prompt may contain.
3. **EV-015.1 with EV-015.2.** A derive job can have an `ok` row with `error_count > 0` in the last 24 hours. That row is a check 5 hit. The same job can also be fresh under check 6, because an `ok` row counts there. The plan doesn't say whether such a job is reported once or under both checks. It also doesn't say whether an `ok` row with errors counts as the `ok` row that satisfies `deriveStaleHours`.
4. **EV-015.2 with EV-015.3.** EV-015.3 exempts `knownIssues` sources from the healthy line. EV-015.2 has no `knownIssues` handling for derive jobs. A job that has never run produces no row, yet EV-015.3 says "a row from either leads the report". The plan doesn't say whether a derive job listed in `knownIssues` is exempt from check 6. It also doesn't say how a never-ran job leads the report.
