# Spec review: DESIGN.md CC-002

_2026-11-18T04:29:38+00:00 · claude-sonnet-5-5 with claude-fable-5-2 advisor (1 calls)_

I found no forced contradictions, so every finding below is an ambiguity. All of them are scoped to the item, and I found no conflict among the constraints alone.

Ambiguities, one line each:
- A1: CC-002.4 (`confirm --retention <class>`) and CC-002.7 ("not a role or a date"). The allowed values for `<class>` aren't stated, so a role-shaped class can't be ruled out.
- A2: CC-002.7 ("no ledger line ever holds a role without its matching date") and CC-002.7 (held-out has no date). It isn't clear whether "none" counts as a matching date.
- A3: CC-002.10 (`status` exits 0 or 3) and CC-002.8 (`status` runs `verify` first, which exits non-zero on a conflict copy). The `status` exit code in that case isn't defined.
- A4: CC-002.10 ("published" plus an active state) against CC-002.5 and CC-002.8. A case can be published with its `add` still unapplied, and it then has no role or state.
- A5: CC-002.10 (held-out share uses the "active held-out" count against the "eligible" total) and CC-002.5 (several `add`s in one `apply`). The inputs to the quarter rule aren't defined.
- A6: CC-002.7 and CC-002.10 on `retire`. It isn't stated whether a proposal case can be retired, or what its date becomes.
- A7: CC-002.4 (second gitleaks pass over the confirmed manifest), the Links section (`publish` records the publication time in the manifest) and *Transcript-derived data* ("write-once manifest"). It isn't clear when the manifest is final.
- A8: CC-002.4 (`--by` is the person who ran the deliberation) and *Transcript-derived data* ("the owner confirms"). It isn't clear whether the owner is Dorian or the deliberator.
- A9: CC-002.4 (source-identity refusal covers only excluded, retired and deleted entries) and CC-002.2 (a re-redacted restage gets a new id). An active case can be restaged and published a second time under a new id.
- A10: CC-002.12 (`run_all_tests.py` "names" the new test) and *The pre-merge gate* (`run_all_tests.py` is a `CODEOWNERS` path). It isn't clear whether the item has to edit that file.
- A11: CC-002.10 (readiness counts and `ready` output) and *Test the boundaries before adding item IDs*. It isn't clear whether this is a "new measurement report or score".
- A12: CC-002.4 (the manifest gitleaks pass runs inside `publish`) and *Transcript-derived data* (gitleaks "run separately"). It isn't clear whether an in-`publish` pass counts as run separately.
