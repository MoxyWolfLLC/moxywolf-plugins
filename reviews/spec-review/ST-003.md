I found no contradictions in ST-003. Every pair I checked can be satisfied together, and the plan's own constraints don't force a conflict anywhere. I found five ambiguities, listed below.

- **ST-003.7 and ST-003.5:** For the Chief of Staff, the report line "ends its report to the person who ran it", and the intro and criterion 7 put the line in the run log "never in a result or memo to Dorian". Dorian is the person who runs it. The plan does not say whether the run log counts as that report.
- **ST-003.3:** "STE-flavored mode" is never defined, so it is unclear how it differs from "Strict mode".
- **ST-003.3 and ST-003.6:** `feedback_*.md` rule text is often Dorian's own words. The plan does not say whether such text is rewritten under criterion 3 or kept verbatim under criterion 6.
- **ST-003.7 and ST-003.10:** Nothing reads the line and no test covers it, so the plan gives no way to decide that criterion 7 is met.
- **ST-003.7:** A run with nothing to rewrite could print `0 rewritten, 0 kept as-is` or print no line. Criterion 7 says "no line means the pass didn't run", so the two cases differ in meaning.
