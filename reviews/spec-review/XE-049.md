I found no contradictions. Each of the advisor's five candidates has a reading where both sides hold, so I recorded them as ambiguities or dropped them.

**Candidates downgraded or dropped**
- **XE-049.7 and XE-049.11 (Amendments lines):** Both can hold, because a non-`light` sync delta just triggers a fix-verification round. It is an ambiguity about whether the review carry-over ever applies.
- **XE-049.4 and XE-049.10 (width):** Whether items overlap depends on what `DEPS.json` `writes` lists. The plan doesn't fix that, so it is an ambiguity.
- **XE-049.2 and XE-049.6 (who merges):** "He makes that merge" is a specific rule for the "merge as it stands" case, so it sits alongside the lane-wide bot merge. Dropped.
- **XE-049.2 and XE-049.11 (further reviews):** XE-049.11 says the sync rounds are separate from `--max-rounds` and caps them at three. Dropped.
- **XE-049.17 and *Transcript-derived data*:** This turns on what "executing principal" means, so it is an ambiguity.

**Ambiguities**
- A1: XE-049.4 and XE-049.5. A goal item's done line names the goal's pull request number, but items commit to `goal/<id>` before any pull request exists.
- A2: XE-049.4 and XE-049.10. Every item edits `DESIGN.md`, and criterion 10 says items with overlapping `writes` serialize. The plan doesn't say the `DESIGN.md` and version exemption from criterion 11 applies to goals.
- A3: XE-049.7 and XE-049.11. Every sibling touches an Amendments line, and an Amendments line is never `light`. The review carry-over may therefore never apply.
- A4: XE-049.11 and *The pre-merge gate*. A `CODEOWNERS` change needs Dorian's approving review "at the head". The plan doesn't say whether that approval carries across a `light` sync delta.
- A5: XE-049.1 and XE-049.11. "Regression" is defined by "the fix's diff", but a sync round is "limited to the sync delta", which has no fix diff.
- A6: XE-049.6, XE-049.9 and *A goal delegates item selection*. "No successor starts without his word" conflicts in wording with "`goal_successor.py` refuses to start one". The plan also keeps describing the unowned successor folder as live.
- A7: XE-049.16 and XE-049.11. XE-049.16 refers to "criterion 11's batch" question, but criterion 11 states no such question.
- A8: XE-049.16 (twice). After approval the coordinator changes a builder's brief "without editing `goals/<id>/`", and the plan doesn't say where that brief lives. The text also says an entry "never becomes a separate stop" and then says it stops the launch as a GO-006 escalation.
- A9: XE-049.17 and *Transcript-derived data*. A later same-family builder on the same account reads the transcript-derived answer before the publish gate succeeds. The plan doesn't define "executing principal".
- A10: XE-049.7 and *The approval ledger is attention, not containment*. "Clear check ledger" stands in for review, and the plan doesn't define it. If it is the approval ledger, it would be claimed as gating a merge.
- A11: XE-049.5, XE-049.13 and XE-049.18. The run record is committed before the whole-goal review with "spend outcomes", but the review and later model calls spend afterwards. The holdout timing is unclear for the same reason.
