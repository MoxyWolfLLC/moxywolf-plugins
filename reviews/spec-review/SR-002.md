I found no contradiction that survives the plan's own carve-outs, and five ambiguities. I read the constraints against each other and against SR-002.

**Checked and consistent:**
- The coverage-exception route and *One release path* agree.
- The agent-tooling carve-out and the `goal_*` list of `CODEOWNERS` paths agree.
- The closed-lane and wide-lane goal merge rules agree.
- Adding `released` and `merged_unreviewed` to the vocabulary is explicitly ruled allowed in *Test the boundaries before adding item IDs*.
- The six-run cap and "each … ends in an edit" don't conflict, because the "except any still open at the six-run cap" clause covers the case.

**Ambiguities:**
- **A1, SR-002.4.** The criterion reads an entry as `item` when its `requirements` name the reviewed item's id. It also says an id a constraint's own text cites, such as GA-011, "doesn't count". Suppose the reviewed item is GA-011 and the entry quotes that constraint. The text doesn't say whether the id counts. The scope label and whether the item's `check` fails both depend on the answer.
- **A2, SR-002.2, SR-002.3 and SR-002.7.** An edit after the stop "restarts the count within the same six runs", yet the sweep "ends" at six. Suppose all six runs are spent and Dorian then rules on a seed with an edit. The record goes stale, and under SR-002.5 the item's `constraints` contradictions then block. The text doesn't say whether a further run is allowed. It also doesn't say what "After the sweep" means for ME-004's re-run when the sweep can restart.
- **A3, SR-002 preamble and *Test the boundaries before adding item IDs*.** The preamble grants a waiver "covering SR-002 only". The constraint, as narrowed, says "Any other new item needs no waiver". It also bars new "measurement report" work, and "a field an item adds to its own record is none of these". The plan doesn't say whether `constraints.json` and `constraints.md` count as a new measurement report. It also doesn't say whether the waiver is needed or already moot.
- **A4, *One release path, checked by code*.** The plan says PR #24's review state "was never established", and PRs #71 and #73 merged with "no review and no recorded exception". It doesn't say what status the items behind them hold. A script can't decide a state that was never established.
- **A5, *One release path, checked by code* and *Test the boundaries before adding item IDs*.** The vocabulary words arrive "with the first fix B-a produces", but B-a's evidence is already recorded. The "Until then" interim rules therefore have no testable end.
