- **PRs #24, #71, #73 (A4):** *One release path* says what happened to each, but never states which status vocabulary word each one holds.
- **Exception "in his words" (A5):** *One release path* says each move has "one condition a script can check". *Coverage is mandatory* makes "the owner's exception, in his words" a condition of the review-to-released move. A script can't check "in his words" unless the plan defines a format.
- **Cited ids in SR-002.4 (A6):** "An id a constraint's own text cites, such as GA-011, doesn't count" is unclear when the reviewed item is itself GA-011.
- **End-of-sweep state (A7):** SR-002.2 says "each ends in an edit" and also lets the sweep stop at six runs with contradictions left over. The state of `check --constraints` is undefined when Dorian edits after the last run. Criterion 5's rule for a stale or open record depends on that state.
