I agree with the advisor's list and have added its two extra items. The empty contradictions list stands. Every issue I found is an ambiguity in GR-002 or at its edge.

- GR-002 AC4 ↔ AC2: AC4 says it applies "AC2's stated rule", but AC2 is the key-matching rule. The compare rule is in AC4 itself.
- GR-002 AC4 ↔ AC3: AC4 compares budget, deadline, draft-to-decision time and decisions sent to Dorian. The AC3 report lists none of these, and it must be byte-identical for the same inputs.
- GR-002 AC4: "same sound decision" doesn't say what makes a decision sound.
- GR-002 AC4: the output says "pass or fail", but reports with different manifests or limits are "refused, not scored". The plan doesn't say whether a refusal counts as a fail or is a separate outcome.
- GR-002 AC3 ↔ AC2: "asks for a change" can't be decided by a matcher that reads no model.
- GR-002 AC2 ↔ Links introduced: the Links paragraph says the key's `condition` makes a finding match. AC2 matches on `evidence_required` only, and `condition` is prose. AC2 doesn't say whether class is part of the match.
- GR-002 AC3: the one-to-one assignment rule is undefined when one finding fits several entries.
- GR-002 AC5: the plan doesn't say which packet holds the open finding: "the next recheck packet or … the packet for Dorian".
- GR-002 AC5 ↔ *The approval ledger is attention, not containment*: AC5 has GR-001's ledger decide whether the Council call is made, so it gates an action. The constraint says no item may claim the ledger stops anything. The plan never says whether GR-001's dispatch ledger is the approval ledger. If it is, this is a contradiction. If not, there is no conflict.
