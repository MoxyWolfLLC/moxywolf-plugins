I found no contradictions in GO-025 or its constraints. I checked the criteria against each other and against the ownership, release and "test the boundaries" constraints. GO-025 only edits `goal_run.py`, which isn't a `CODEOWNERS` path, and it leaves `swarm.py` and `hooks/hooks.json` alone. It adds no vocabulary, metrics or graph work, so the boundary-test constraint doesn't apply.

The ambiguities are below. I left out the advisor's last item, about missing test cases (GO-025.4 and GO-025.1). A test list that stops short of every case isn't a requirement that can't be decided.

- **GO-025.1:** "No `outcome`" could mean the key is absent, or also null or empty. The plan also doesn't say what happens when `state.json` is unreadable.
- **GO-025.2 and GO-025.3:** `swarm.py` can't change, so it must already have `viewer-start` and `viewer-stop` with the outputs GO-025.2 relays. The plan doesn't say whether it does.
- **Links introduced and GO-025.1:** "Running" is defined two ways: no `outcome`, or no `outcome` plus an existing `swarm/` folder. GO-025.2's "several running goals" and "none running" don't say which one they mean.
- **GO-025.2:** The plan doesn't say whether a first argument of `stop` is always the verb. It also doesn't say what `stop` with no id does when the only leftover viewer belongs to an ended goal.
- **GO-025.5:** The top-level marketplace version "moves" to no stated value.
- **GO-025.5 and "Reuse existing governance and audit machinery":** "By text replace" could mean bypassing `version_bump.py` or using it.
