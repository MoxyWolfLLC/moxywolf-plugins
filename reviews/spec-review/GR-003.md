I found no contradictions. Five requirement pairs are ambiguous, and all of them involve GR-003.

- **Links vs. boundary tests.** "Links introduced: two" in GR-003 and GR-003.2 may or may not count as "graph work" or "vocabulary work" under *Test the boundaries before adding item IDs*. That constraint exempts "a field an item adds to its own record", but the plan doesn't say whether these links fall under the exemption. The class values `false_premise`, `wording_defect` and `behavior_gap` raise the same question.
- **Transcript-derived data.** GR-003.4 attaches "the same pinned evidence for every lane". The plan doesn't say whether that evidence can come from session transcripts. If it can, *Transcript-derived data isn't readable outside the executing principal until the publish gate succeeds* applies, and preflight may not be allowed to attach it.
- **`run_all_tests.py` ownership.** GR-003.8 requires `run_all_tests.py` to be green in CI. The plan doesn't say whether registering `test_review_lessons.py` means editing `run_all_tests.py`. That file is a `CODEOWNERS` path, so editing it would need Dorian's approving review. That conflicts with the item merging autonomously under *The pre-merge gate is GitHub's, sized to the stakes*.
- **Retire condition.** GR-003.2 stores a "retire condition", and GR-003.4 loads a lesson only when it "is not met". The plan doesn't say how a script decides that.
- **Class of a false alarm.** GR-003.2 requires one of three classes, and GR-003.3 keeps "a verified false alarm". The plan doesn't say which class a false alarm gets, or whether it needs a fourth one.
