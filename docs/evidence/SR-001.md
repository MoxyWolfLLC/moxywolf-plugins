## The executor's report (`all.md`)

Item 1 and Item 3 contradict each other. The two ambiguities are real but smaller.

**C1: Item 1 and Item 3.** Item 1 says the export "makes no network request of any kind, so it works on a plane." Item 3 says that before writing each row the export "fetches that day's exchange rate from the bank's live rates API." Every export, and every row in it, therefore makes a network request. Both cannot hold in any scenario. The smallest fix is to change Item 3 so it needs no live call. Either read rates from a local rates table that is bundled or cached, or drop the USD column. Alternatively, Item 1 could be relaxed to allow that one call, but then the "works on a plane" goal is lost.

**A1: Item 3.** "That day's exchange rate" could mean the invoice date or the export date. The choice changes every USD value, and the plan's text doesn't settle it.

**A2: Item 4.** "YYYY-MM" could mean the month being exported or the month the export runs. The two differ whenever Finance exports a prior month.

I found no conflict between Items 2 and 3, because the native-currency column and the USD column can sit side by side. Items 4 and 5 raise no further issues.
