# Professor Skill - Quick Start Guide

## What This Skill Does

The Professor Skill performs comprehensive academic peer review on research papers, providing:

- **10-phase systematic critique** from AI detection to contribution assessment
- **Brutal honesty** about what's wrong (like Reviewer 2)
- **Constructive guidance** with detailed improvement plans
- **Publication readiness** assessment with rejection risk prediction

## When to Use

- Before submitting a paper to a conference or journal
- When you've received "revise and resubmit" and need a roadmap
- To identify fatal flaws before reviewers do
- To get honest assessment of publication chances

## How to Use

### Basic Usage

```
I have a research paper I'd like you to critique using the professor skill.
[Upload paper as .docx or .pdf]
```

The skill will automatically:
1. Detect AI authorship patterns
2. Verify document integrity
3. Analyze citations and references
4. Evaluate methodology and logic
5. Assess contribution and novelty
6. Provide detailed improvement plan

### Advanced Usage

**Focus on specific aspects**:
```
Using the professor skill, focus on the reference completeness and methodology sections of this paper.
```

**Request specific output format**:
```
Critique this paper using the professor skill, and give me just the executive summary and path forward.
```

**Compare versions**:
```
I've revised the paper based on your feedback. Can you re-critique it and show improvements?
```

## What You'll Get

### 1. Executive Summary
- Overall verdict (Accept/Revise/Reject)
- Key strengths and weaknesses
- Grade (A+ to F)

### 2. Phase-by-Phase Analysis
- **Phase 1**: AI Detection - Is this human-written?
- **Phase 2**: Document Integrity - Are all tables/figures present?
- **Phase 3**: Citation Format - Is it consistent?
- **Phase 4**: Reference Completeness - Are all sources verifiable?
- **Phase 5**: Logic & Argumentation - Are claims sound?
- **Phase 6**: Methodological Rigor - Is the research valid?
- **Phase 7**: Literature Review - Is coverage comprehensive?
- **Phase 8**: Evidence & Results - Are claims supported?
- **Phase 9**: Writing Quality - Is it well-written?
- **Phase 10**: Contribution Assessment - Is it novel?

### 3. Detailed Path Forward
- Week-by-week improvement plan
- Specific action items (not generic advice)
- Resource requirements and budget
- Timeline to publication
- Target venues

### 4. Reviewer Prediction
- What Reviewer 1, 2, 3 will likely say
- Meta-review prediction
- Rejection risk assessment

## Key Improvements Over Previous Versions

### ✓ AI Detection First
The skill now ALWAYS checks for AI authorship patterns first, as this affects interpretation of everything else.

### ✓ Flexible Citation Criteria
Identifies which citation style is being used (Vancouver, APA, etc.) rather than imposing one standard.

### ✓ Comprehensive Verifiability
Accepts URL, DOI, ISBN, arXiv, or Patent Number as valid - doesn't demand DOIs for everything.

### ✓ Enumerated Evidence
Every quantitative claim ("X% of references incomplete") is backed by complete enumeration of examples.

### ✓ Actionable Path Forward
No generic advice like "add evaluation" - instead: "Implement Claimify baseline on Federal Register corpus with Precision/Recall/F1 metrics over 100 annotated documents."

## Example Use Cases

### Case 1: First Draft Assessment
**Situation**: You've written a paper and want to know if it's ready to submit.

**What to do**: Upload the draft and ask for a full critique.

**What you'll get**: Honest assessment of current quality, list of critical fixes needed, timeline to submission-ready state.

### Case 2: Reviewer Response Planning
**Situation**: You got "major revision" and need to address reviewer comments.

**What to do**: Upload the paper with reviewer comments and ask how to address them.

**What you'll get**: Prioritized response plan, specific experiments/analyses to add, rebuttal strategy.

### Case 3: Pre-Submission Check
**Situation**: Paper feels ready, but you want one final check before submission.

**What to do**: Request a focused critique on methodology and references.

**What you'll get**: Validation of readiness or identification of last-minute issues.

## What Makes This Skill Different

- **Writing skills**: Generative and supportive (create content)

### vs. Grammar Checkers
- **Professor**: Assesses research contribution and validity

### vs. Human Reviewers
- **Professor**: Available 24/7, unlimited revisions, constructive

## Common Questions

### Q: Will this skill write my paper for me?
**A**: No. This skill critiques existing papers. Use writing skills (academia-formatting, content-research-writer) for creation.

### Q: Does it work for all academic fields?
**A**: Yes, but strongest for empirical research in CS, engineering, and sciences. May need calibration for humanities.

### Q: Can it check if my paper will be accepted?
**A**: It predicts rejection risk and likely reviewer scores, but actual acceptance depends on reviewers and venue standards.

### Q: Should I follow all recommendations?
**A**: Prioritize CRITICAL fixes first. MAJOR improvements strengthen the paper. MINOR polish is optional.

### Q: Can I use this for grant proposals or thesis chapters?
**A**: Yes! The methodology applies to any research document.

## Tips for Best Results

### 1. Upload Complete Drafts
- Include all sections (even if incomplete)
- Include references section
- Include tables and figures

### 2. Specify Target Venue (Optional)
Tell the skill if you're targeting:
- Top-tier conference (NeurIPS, ICML, ACL)
- Second-tier venue
- Journal
- Workshop

This calibrates expectations.

### 3. Ask Specific Questions
Instead of: "Is this good?"
Try: "What are the 3 most critical flaws that would cause rejection?"

### 4. Iterate
After implementing fixes, re-submit for validation:
"I've addressed the reference completeness issues. Can you re-check Phase 4?"

## Expected Timeline

**For typical paper**:
- Initial critique: 5-10 minutes
- Detailed path forward: Included
- Implementation of critical fixes: 1-2 weeks
- Implementation of major improvements: 2-8 weeks
- Full revision cycle: 2-4 months

## Success Metrics

Papers that follow the detailed path forward typically:
- Move from C/D grades to B+/A- grades
- Reduce rejection risk from 70%+ to <30%
- Receive "minor revision" or "accept" on first submission
- Get higher reviewer scores (4-5/5 instead of 2-3/5)

## Limitations

### What This Skill Can't Do
- Write new content (use writing skills for that)
- Access paywalled papers to verify citations
- Submit your paper to journals
- Guarantee acceptance (only reviewers decide)

### What May Need Human Judgment
- Domain-specific evaluation criteria
- Highly specialized methodology questions
- Political/sensitive research topics
- Interdisciplinary work spanning multiple fields
