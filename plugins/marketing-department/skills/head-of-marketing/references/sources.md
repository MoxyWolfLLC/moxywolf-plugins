## CAN-SPAM Act: A Compliance Guide for Business

Federal Trade Commission · US · public domain (US government), quote freely

<https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business>

Source date: August 2023, edited January 2024. Retrieved 2026-10-07.

**Authoritative for:** What a commercial email has to carry and honor: accurate header and routing information, a subject line that matches the content, identification as an advertisement, a valid physical postal address, a clear way to opt out, and opt-outs honored within 10 business days. It also says the sender stays responsible for email sent on its behalf. Use it to shape a draft. Whether one specific email complies is a legal judgment, and that goes to legal.

## 16 CFR Part 255: Guides Concerning Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising

Federal Trade Commission, via the Electronic Code of Federal Regulations · US · public domain (US government), quote freely

<https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-255>

Source date: eCFR text up to date as of 2026-10-05. Retrieved 2026-10-07.

**Authoritative for:** When a testimonial, a customer quote, an expert's word or an organization's endorsement can be used in marketing, and when a material connection has to be disclosed (sections 255.0 to 255.6). Use it to flag a draft that leans on an endorsement. Whether a given use is lawful goes to legal.

## FTC Policy Statement Regarding Advertising Substantiation

Federal Trade Commission · US · public domain (US government), quote freely

<https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation>

Source date: 1984-11-23. Retrieved 2026-10-07.

**Authoritative for:** The rule that an advertiser needs a reasonable basis for an objective claim before the claim runs. It's why a product, performance or comparative claim in a draft carries its evidence beside it, and why a claim without any stays flagged. What counts as a reasonable basis for a particular claim goes to legal.
